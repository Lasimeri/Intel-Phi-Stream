# 2026-10-02: the lens aside against a placebo (guide ab)

The question (`src/engine.md`, the guide lane): does an aside made of
the line's J-space words ("on my mind: ...", the words the lens reads but
the line does not say) move the stream's next token more than the same
frame filled with words the line did say? If not, the lens carries
nothing the frame does not, and it stays a display (`tui.md`).

The rule, written before measuring (`src/engine.md`): with at least 1500
thinking tokens per arm, the lens must move a token by at least 1.5 times
placebo's mean KL and flip the likeliest token at least 3 points more
often. Only then may a mixing test follow.

## The run

- Service: the dev launch with `--guide`, `guide ab` (lens and placebo
  forks by turns, one per fork, so no window favours one arm).
- Window: 13:13:37 to 13:41:39 local (`since-ab` to the last `guide.log`
  line), on Qwen3.8-35B-A3B Q6_K over the split, llama.cpp unchanged.
- Read with `tools/guide-analyze START_US END_US guide.log --src lens`
  (and `--src placebo`); the log is `~/.local/share/phi-stream/dev/guide.log`.

| arm | thinking tokens | mean KL (nats) | median KL | flips |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| lens | 3622 | 0.0143 | 0.0008 | 93 (2.6 %) |
| placebo | 1558 | 0.0177 | 0.0014 | 40 (2.6 %) |

## Verdict

Both arms passed 1500 thinking tokens. The lens moved the next token 0.81
times as much as the placebo, against the 1.5 the rule asked, and flipped
the likeliest token exactly as often. The rule fails on both counts: the
J-space words, given as an aside, do nothing that a line of the stream's
own words does not. The lens stays in the terminal (the REASONING pane's
"on its mind" rows) and is not mixed into the choice; `guide-mix` is not
run for it.

Caveat: the arms are unequal in tokens (the cause is not measured here),
and one run of 28 minutes on one stream is one sample of its work. The difference in KL is small
in both directions; what is decided is only that the lens shows no
advantage of the size the rule required.
